CHLAMYDOSAURUS KINGII

Frilled-Neck Lizard

The frilled-neck lizard is famous for the large fold of skin around its neck. When threatened, it can open its mouth and spread its frill to make itself appear much larger.

Quick Facts

Location: Northern Australia and New Guinea

Diet: Insects and small animals

Known For: Its dramatic neck frill

CHRYSOPELEA

Flying Snake

Flying snakes do not actually fly. Instead, they flatten their bodies and glide through the air from tree to tree.

Quick Facts

Location: South and Southeast Asia

Diet: Lizards, frogs and small animals

Known For: Gliding between trees

CHAMAELEONIDAE

Chameleon

Chameleons are known for their unusual eyes, long tongues and ability to change their appearance.

Quick Facts

Location: Africa, Madagascar, Asia and Europe

Diet: Mostly insects

Known For: Color changes and independent eyes
