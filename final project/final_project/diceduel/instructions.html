🎲 DICE DUEL INSTRUCTIONS

Learn the rules, understand the payouts, and get ready to challenge the house.

▶ PLAY DICE DUEL

How to Play

  1. Choose your wager. Enter a positive whole-number amount in the wager box, or select a quick-bet button. Your wager cannot exceed your available credits.
  2. Roll the dice. Select Roll the Dice. You and the house each roll one six-sided die.
  3. Compare the numbers. If your roll is higher than the house's roll, you win. If the rolls match, your wager is returned. If the house rolls higher, you lose your wager.
  4. Check your result. The game displays both rolls, your total payout, and your net profit or loss. Your shared balance and statistics update automatically.
  5. Play another round. Keep your wager or enter a new amount, then select Roll the Dice again.

Each die independently generates a random number from 1 through 6. Previous rounds do not affect the next roll.

🏆 Rules and Payouts

All payout multipliers include your original wager.
Result Total Payout Net Change
Your roll is higher 2× your wager Gain your wager amount
The rolls match 1× your wager No change
The house rolls higher 0 credits Lose your wager amount

🎲 Example Rounds

Each example below starts with a balance of 5,000 credits and a wager of 100 credits.

You Win

You roll 5 and the house rolls 2. Your total payout is 200 credits, including your original 100-credit wager. Your net profit is 100 credits, leaving a balance of 5,100 credits.

A Tie

You and the house both roll 4. Your 100-credit wager is returned. Your balance stays at 5,000 credits.

The House Wins

You roll 2 and the house rolls 6. Your payout is 0 credits. You lose your 100-credit wager, leaving a balance of 4,900 credits.

Your Wallet and Statistics

  • Shared wallet: Dice Duel uses the same credits as Coin Flip, Neon Slots, and your other Neon Empire games.
  • Games Played: Every completed round counts as one game, including ties.
  • Total Wins: Your win count increases when your roll beats the house. Ties do not count as wins.
  • Player Level: You start at Level 1 and gain one level for every 10 completed games across the casino.
  • Saved progress: Your balance and statistics are saved in this browser for this site.

All wagers use fictional credits. No real money or prizes.
