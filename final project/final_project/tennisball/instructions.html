How to Play

  1. Choose your wager. Enter at least 5 credits in multiples of 5, or select a quick-bet button. Your wager cannot exceed your available balance.
  2. Select one cup. A tennis ball is randomly hidden under one of three cups. Selecting a cup immediately places your wager.
  3. Reveal the result. All three cups reveal their contents. If your selected cup contains the tennis ball, you win. If it is empty, you lose your wager.
  4. Check your payout. A correct choice returns 2.8 times your wager, including the original wager. An incorrect choice returns 0 credits.
  5. Prepare another round. Select Play Again to cover the cups. You can then change your wager and make another choice. Selecting Play Again does not charge credits.

Each cup has an equal 1 in 3 chance of hiding the ball. Every round uses a new random position, so previous results do not affect the next round.

This is a guessing game. There is no cup-shuffling sequence to track.
